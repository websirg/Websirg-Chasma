Medical Precision First

The Bhardwaj Clinical Standard

Eyewear is not merely cosmetic. It is an ocular prescription resting right before the visual cortex. A minor error of 0.25 cylinder or 1.5mm in pupillary distance (PD) leads to chronic eyestrain, migraines, and visual distortion.

At Bhardwaj Chasma Ghar, every refraction is certified by experienced ophthalmologists, and every lens is custom-cut using CNC diamond edging wheels verified by automated digital lensometers.
