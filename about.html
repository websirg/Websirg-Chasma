The Bhardwaj Clinical Standard

Eyewear is not merely cosmetic. It is an ocular prescription resting right before the visual cortex. A minor error of 0.25 cylinder or inaccurate pupillary distance (PD) leads to chronic eyestrain, headaches, and visual distortion.

At Bhardwaj Chasma Ghar, eye examinations are conducted with dedicated care, and every prescription chasma is custom-fitted with automated optical accuracy.
