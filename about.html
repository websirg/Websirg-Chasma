Our Heritage & Mission

Three Decades of Precision Vision & Eye Care

Founded in Kanpur, Bhardwaj Chasma Ghar bridges the critical gap between medical clinical ophthalmology and artistic optical dispensing.

The Bhardwaj Philosophy

Eyewear is not simply a fashion accessory; it is a precision medical device resting directly in front of the human eye. An inaccurate millimeter in pupillary distance (PD) or a 2-degree distortion in astigmatic axis can cause chronic headaches, dizziness, and visual fatigue.

Under the clinical direction of Dr. Alok Bhardwaj (MS Ophthalmology) and dispensing leadership of Rajeev Bhardwaj, every pair of glasses crafted in our workshop undergoes stringent double-verification against computerized lensometers.
