Patient Education Guide

Ophthalmic Surgeries & Eye Procedures Overview

Transparent, evidence-based clinical information about advanced anterior segment surgeries and laser vision correction.

1. Micro-Incision Cataract Phacoemulsification (MICS)

Day-Care Procedure

Cataracts occur when the natural crystalline lens becomes opaque over time. Modern MICS cataract surgery employs high-frequency ultrasound energy through a self-sealing 1.8mm–2.2mm incision to gently emulsify the cloudy lens, replacing it with an advanced foldable Intraocular Lens (IOL) such as Monofocal, Toric (astigmatism correction), or Trifocal/EDOF lenses.

Post-Surgical Care: Fast recovery within 24–48 hours. Patients can select personalized post-operative reading spectacles from our optical dispensary.

2. Refractive Vision Correction Evaluation (LASIK / PRK / ICL)

Glasses Removal Assessment

For young adults seeking freedom from spectacle dependence, our ophthalmic team performs comprehensive corneal topography (pachymetry) and anterior chamber depth scanning. We determine whether the eye is an ideal candidate for femtosecond blade-free LASIK, surface PRK, or Implantable Collamer Lens (ICL) for high myopic powers.

Clinical Principle: Not every cornea is eligible for laser surgery. We prioritize absolute corneal safety and recommend custom thin-index eyewear when laser is contraindicated.

3. Pterygium Excision with Conjunctival Autograft

Ocular Surface Surgery

Pterygium (Surfer’s Eye) is a benign fleshy growth over the cornea induced by UV exposure and dry dust in the Indian climate. Modern suture-less microsurgery uses fibrin tissue glue to transfer a microscopic healthy graft from the upper conjunctiva, drastically reducing recurrence rates to under 2%.

4. Diabetic Retinopathy & Glaucoma Laser Management

Retina & Optic Nerve Care

Diabetic patients require annual dilated fundus examinations. When micro-aneurysms or macular edema develop, timely selective laser therapy or anti-VEGF injections prevent permanent irreversible vision loss.

* Note: Educational guide only. Individual treatment is determined strictly through in-person slit lamp clinical evaluation.
